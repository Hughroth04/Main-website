Why The Website?

Honestly, the only reason I have this website is because I wanted to learn HTML, so this is me doing that. I am hoping to utilize it for ministry purposes, specifically evangelism resources as I continue to learn how to teach evangelism. I would also like to use this page as an opportunity for anybody in associated ministry to get to know me briefly through reading about my calling.

My Ministry

I surrendered to ministry in 2022 after turning 18, in doing this I really did not know what exactly that entailed. I knew God was calling me to serve Him in a unique capacity and up until recently I had zero idea what God was calling me to. Before explaining my calling, I would like to note that I am beyond thankful to be called by the creator of the universe to serve in ministry. There is no higher honor. I am a broken sinner in need of Jesus, and am not putting this content on my website to gain publicity, but instead to give a personal insight to anyone who is interested. As a young preacher, it is important to me that people know my heart before deciding to endorse me in any way. Now, let's shift gears. God has called me to serve as an evangelist, and my burden is for the 70% of Christians who have never shared the Gospel with a stranger. I cannot say what I will be doing 20 years from now, however in this season of my life, God has called me to pour into others and help equip them to share Jesus daily. I have a lot in mind as far as how I would like to do this in the future, but at the moment I am an evangelist sent out by Alvin Missionary Baptist Church in Alvin, TX. I host small evangelism outreaches, and am currently the director of the Outreach Bible Project for the state of Texas. I am the social media coordinator for the OBP but have also been blessed with opportunities to help cultivate the training, and systems that encourage growth of our attendees in their daily walk beyond the outreach events. I am beyond thankful to be a part of the Outreach Bible Project, and it has been a huge part of my development as an evangelist.

My career

If you are reading this, you may be my grandma, a fellow church member, maybe you are a co worker, or someone looking to do business. If I could do ministry full time, I would. But that is not realistic at the moment. I am a student, and will get married soon. I am marketing coordinator for Green Country Pest Control, I do social media marketing, email marketing, videography, photography, graphic design, inside sales, and a little bit of outside sales. I do this part time and this job has helped me grow in many ways that benefit my ministry goals. I am thankful to say that my employer is very patient and allows me to go on quite a bit of trips for ministry with no consequences. Long term, I would love to be all in on ministry as far as time allocation goes, however I am aware that I will be working in this field for a while longer until God leads me to a place of full time ministry. I am looking forward to the future God has for me, but for now am going to strive to be a good steward of the job situation God has blessed me with.
